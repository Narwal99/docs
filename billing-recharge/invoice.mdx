## Tax Profile

GPUhub is a Singapore business and is required to comply with applicable Singapore tax laws and regulations. Choose the tax profile that matches the party making the payment. Tax profile information is used for billing, tax calculation, and invoice issuance. Please provide complete and accurate information that reflects the actual purchaser.

<Note>
Effective October 5, 2026, at 12:00 AM (UTC+8), a 9% Goods and Services Tax (GST) will be charged to customers in Singapore. GST applicability is determined using the billing and tax information in your Tax Profile. Please do not use placeholder, inaccurate, or another party's information.
</Note>

### Tax Profile - Individual
Choose **Individual** when you are purchasing services for personal use. Enter your full legal name and billing address exactly as they appear on your official records.

![](/images/invoice-title-1.png)

### Tax Profile- Business

Choose **Business** when you are purchasing services on behalf of a company or other legal entity. Enter the registered legal entity name, registered billing address, and applicable business registration or tax identification number. You must be authorized to provide this information on behalf of the business.

Use real, current information for every Tax Profile. Incorrect or incomplete details may delay invoice processing or result in an inaccurate tax calculation.

![](/images/invoice-title-2.png)
